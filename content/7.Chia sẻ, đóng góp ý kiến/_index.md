## General Impressions and Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

During my research and implementation of the project **“Design and Deployment of a Secure Web Server on AWS”**, I had the opportunity to experience a learning environment that emphasized initiative, hands-on practice, and independent research.

In addition to studying theoretical concepts, I learned how to apply my knowledge to a specific problem, from designing the architecture to configuring, testing, and documenting the deployment results.

During my internship, I found the working environment friendly, open, and supportive of interns who wanted to learn proactively. I was able to focus on my assigned tasks while also having the opportunity to independently explore AWS topics that I had never studied before. What I appreciated most was that the environment did not create excessive pressure, yet still encouraged everyone to be self-disciplined and responsible for their work.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Team Members

During the program, I encountered quite a few challenges related to cloud computing as well as some matters outside the technical scope, and the mentors and other colleagues were always willing to help. In particular, the cloud knowledge they shared during the workshops and while we were developing our own workshop helped me understand that it is not enough simply to make a system work. I also need to be able to explain why specific services were chosen and how those services connect with one another. Through these experiences, I gradually improved my technical thinking and my ability to present a solution clearly.

### 3. Relevance of Practical Work to My Academic Major

I am studying Computer Networks and Communications, so before joining the program, my knowledge mainly focused on computer networks, network administration, and some aspects of computer systems. When I began learning about AWS, I realized that cloud computing involves many concepts related to what I had studied, such as servers, storage, access permissions, networking, and system monitoring. However, their practical implementation introduced many new approaches.

While working on the project **“Design and Deployment of a Secure Web Server on AWS Using”**, I had the opportunity to combine my knowledge of servers and networking with cloud services such as EC2, EBS, S3, IAM, AWS Backup, and CloudWatch. This helped me better understand how knowledge gained at university can be applied in a cloud environment.

### 4. Learning Opportunities and Personal Development

The internship allowed me to gain knowledge in areas I had not previously explored in depth, particularly cloud services. While working on the project, I did more than follow instructions: I also had to research documentation independently, troubleshoot errors, and try different configurations before achieving the desired results.

Through this process, I improved my ability to learn independently, find relevant documentation, analyze problems, and verify results after each deployment step. I also realized that I need to understand the underlying purpose of each service rather than simply memorize the steps performed in the AWS Console.

### 5. Team Spirit and Collaboration

I felt that the working environment placed great importance on initiative and independent learning. Each intern was responsible for managing their own progress, but whenever difficulties arose, we could discuss them and receive support.

This helped me gradually develop the habit of researching a problem on my own first, then summarizing any unresolved issues before discussing them with others. In addition, studying and exchanging ideas with fellow interns gave me new perspectives on problem-solving and motivated me throughout the process of completing the workshop.

### 6. Program Evaluation and Suggestions for Improvement

During my internship, I was given the opportunity to learn new concepts while gaining hands-on experience with AWS. I particularly appreciated that the program did not simply require interns to complete a project, but also included knowledge-sharing sessions, guidance, and reviews that helped us better understand real-world working practices.

The opportunity to explore cloud technologies, work directly with AWS services, and attend knowledge-sharing sessions throughout the project was extremely valuable to me. This internship has also given me a clearer direction for continuing to study AWS, cloud computing, and topics related to my major in Computer Networks and Communications.

As someone who appreciates a pleasant workplace and a positive working culture, I found the office to be an excellent place to work. From the security team to the supportive staff and colleagues, everyone helped create a warm and comfortable atmosphere. It truly felt like a second family to me.
